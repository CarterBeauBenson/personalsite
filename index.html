About

Formal modeling at work. Sports and research after hours.

I’m Carter-Beau Benson, a Project Manager and Ontologist at CUBRC with a PhD in Philosophy from the University at Buffalo.

My professional interests include applied ontology, knowledge representation, semantic interoperability, knowledge graphs, ontology-supported AI, and the management of ontology-driven technical projects.

Outside of professional work, I use baseball and football as test domains for personal ontology and knowledge-graph projects. Sports combine events, roles, rules, measurements, and large public datasets in ways that make them especially useful for experimenting with semantic models.

BASEBALL · ONTOLOGY · KNOWLEDGE GRAPH

BaseballO

↗

BaseballO is an open-source semantic system for representing professional baseball as connected events, participants, evidence, and game context.

The project transforms baseball source data into a persistent RDF knowledge graph that can be queried across games, players, plate appearances, pitches, runner movements, and outcomes.

Its broader goal is to explore reusable queries, auditable statistics, richer connections between events, and analytical questions that are difficult to express in conventional tabular datasets.
