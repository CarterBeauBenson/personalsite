BaseballO

↗

BaseballO is an open-source semantic system for representing professional baseball as connected events, participants, evidence, and game context rather than as a collection of disconnected statistics.

The project transforms baseball source data into a persistent RDF knowledge graph that can be queried across games, players, plate appearances, pitches, runner movements, and outcomes. Instead of storing traditional aggregate statistics as the primary representation, BaseballO models the underlying game events and derives analytical results from them.

The broader goal is to explore what becomes possible when baseball data is given explicit semantics: reusable queries, auditable statistics, richer connections between events, and analytical questions that are difficult to express in conventional tabular datasets.

FutOn

↗

A formal ontology of association football developed alongside my doctoral research. FutOn uses BFO and CCO to represent players, roles, positions, match processes, information entities, and expected goals.
